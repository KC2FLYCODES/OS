The build

Seven layers.
Zero shortcuts.

  1. 01Rafters checkedWe inspect the framing first. Soft or cracked rafters get fixed before anything goes on top.
  2. 02Solid deckingRotten sheathing comes out. New plywood goes down, nailed to spec.
  3. 03Ice & water shieldA self-sealing membrane at the eaves and valleys, where leaks actually start.
  4. 04UnderlaymentA full synthetic layer over the deck, overlapped so water runs off, not in.
  5. 05Drip edge & flashingMetal at every edge, wall, and chimney so water has nowhere to sneak.
  6. 06Shingle coursesStarter strip, then course by course, nailed in the manufacturer's zone.
  7. 07Ridge cap & ventThe top seals it and lets the attic breathe. Then we sweep the yard.
